Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Marble Jam: Sort Factory is a game. It has no accounts, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives and settings) is stored only on your device. We can't see it. The game does not use analytics.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you can choose to watch (for example to continue a level, double your coins or get a life) and, from level 15, occasional ads between levels. Ads never interrupt a puzzle. On first launch the game asks once whether you allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). Only if you choose "Allow" does Google AdMob use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are also shown Google's consent form, which can be reopened from the game's Settings ("Privacy choices"). Google may use limited device-level information, such as approximate region, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads between levels.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to general-audience content.
