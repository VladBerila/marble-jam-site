How do I play?

Tap a tube to release its bottom marble. It drops into the open jar of its colour; if there isn't one, it waits in the tray. A full jar ships and the next jar takes its place, pulling matching marbles out of the tray. If a marble needs the tray and every cup is full, the line jams. The upcoming jars are shown on the conveyor at the bottom, so you can plan ahead.

I'm stuck

Use the boosters: Undo takes back your last tap, +1 Cup adds a tray cup, Hint shows a tube that still leads to a win, and Mix reshuffles the remaining marbles into an order that can still be solved. Every level has been checked by our solver and can be won without boosters.

Lives

From level 11, losing a level (or quitting one you've started) uses a life. Lives refill one every 30 minutes, up to 5. You can also refill them with coins or by watching an optional ad.

I bought something on another device

Open the Shop (tap the coin counter) and tap Restore purchases.

My progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress; No Ads and the Starter Pack can always be restored.
