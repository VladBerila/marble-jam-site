Marble Jam

Tap, drop, sort!

A marble-sorting puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Tap a tube to drop its bottom marble into the jar of its colour, or park it in the small tray. Fill a jar and it ships, and the next jar pulls matching marbles out of the tray for satisfying chains. Plan ahead: when the tray overflows, the line jams. 300 levels with hidden marbles, locked tubes, HARD and SUPER HARD challenges, and Jammy the jar cheering you on.
